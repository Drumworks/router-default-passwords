## What is and is not covered

Covered: this compilation. The selection of models, the verification of each row against
manufacturer documentation, the credential-type classification, the per-row provenance, the
reset instructions and notes written for each model, and the compliance index derived from it.

Not covered, because nobody owns them: the underlying facts. A given router's default gateway
IP, admin username and default password are facts about that product, stated by its own
manufacturer. Facts are not copyrightable. ssid.ai claims no ownership of them and does not
assert exclusivity over them.

Also not covered: the third-party documents cited in `source_url`. Those belong to their
respective manufacturers and are linked, not reproduced.

## No warranty

The dataset is provided as is. Every row is compiled from official manufacturer documentation
and carries the URL it came from, but manufacturers revise their documentation, hardware
revisions of one model name differ, and ISPs re-flash the units they supply. Rows marked

Use this data on equipment you own or administer. If a row is wrong, send a correction with
